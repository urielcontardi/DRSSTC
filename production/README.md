## Enclosure

- [Enclosure v1 package](case/v1/ARCSTRIKE-case-v1-CONCEPT.zip): assembly STEP, STLs, viewer, source files, and instructions.

- [Enclosure v2 package](case/v2/ARCSTRIKE-case-v2-CONCEPT.zip): integrated 60 × 280 mm secondary, 150 × 40 mm toroid, continuous primary spiral, STEP/STLs, SolidCraft assembly and viewer.

- [Enclosure v3 compact package](case/v3/ARCSTRIKE-case-v3-CONCEPT.zip): 130 × 130 × 94 mm rounded body with Ø180 mm circular resonator platform, STEP/STLs, SolidCraft assembly and viewer.

- [Enclosure v4 assembly package](case/v4/ARCSTRIKE-case-v4-ASSEMBLY.zip): guided joints, captive M3/M4 nuts, modeled fasteners, tube clamps, heatsink retention, oriented STLs and fit coupons; primary wire holder deferred.

- [Enclosure v5 adjustable package](case/v5/ARCSTRIKE-case-v5-ASSEMBLY.zip): adjustable heatsink cradle for the supplied 104 × 25 mm profile, height shims, four sliding retainers and rounded fiber connector access with a fit sample.

Current source and limitations: [case v5](../mechanical/case-v5/README.md). Physical fits and thermal/electrical behavior require validation.
