# Adjusted 3D models

- `capacitor_snapin.py`: 30.3 × 40.5 mm bodies with 9 and 10 mm lead pitches.
- `igbt_bottom.py`: underside IGBTs with the metal face toward the heatsink and leads aligned to the existing holes.
- `bridge_trimmed.py`: KBU bridge with leads ending 1 mm below the PCB.

These models preserve the adopted visual approximations and do not verify
physical lead fit. Output: `hardware/pcb/library/3dmodels/`.
See [tool commands](../README.md).
