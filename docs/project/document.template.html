01 / Architecture

What we are building

A DRSSTC with a two-IGBT half bridge, a series primary tank, and a resonant secondary with a toroidal top load. The interrupter signal arrives through optical fiber; a current transformer provides bridge feedback.

AC input → rectifier / DC bus → half bridge → primary tank → coupled secondary + toroid
Optical fiber → logic → gate drivers → GDT

Confirmed in the project

CONFIRMED 100 × 100 mm PCB, CPR1 = 68 nF / 3 kV, external primary between P1/P2, and Q1/Q2 half-bridge IGBTs. Models show underside IGBTs and a rectifier with shortened leads.

Chosen for calculation

PROPOSED 60 mm tube, AWG 36 enameled copper (0.127 mm bare diameter), 1,500 turns, and a 150 × 40 mm toroid. An eight-turn flat primary provides an adjustable tap. Purchased-part dimensions remain unconfirmed.

Bus electrolytics and the resonant capacitor have different functions. Tuning uses CPR1 = 68 nF, rather than the bus capacitance.

03 / Construction guide

Winding the proposed secondary

Wind one continuous layer of fine enameled wire on a rigid tube. These dimensions belong to the calculated metric adaptation.

  1. Check the tube and wire. Measure actual tube OD and confirm both bare copper and finished enamel diameters. The assumed pitch comes from Remington AWG 36 single-build 36SNS, rather than a measurement of the purchased wire.
  2. Mark the winding region. Start 30 mm above the base and finish at 243.36 mm for the assumed pitch. Recalculate this position if the actual pitch differs.
  3. Wind 1,500 turns. Keep turns adjacent without crossings or overlaps. Use a counter and even tension without stretching or damaging the wire.
  4. Secure the ends. Leave connection leads and provide strain relief. Avoid loose conductors hanging inside the tube.
  5. Apply compatible insulating varnish. Follow the product instructions and allow full curing. The original manual calls for varnishing before operation.
  6. Check before power assembly. Verify continuity and resistance, then measure resonance with the toroid fitted using low-signal excitation and leaving the bridge unpowered.
At 0.1524 mm finished wire diameter, the same 1,500 turns occupy 228.60 mm and require new tuning calculations. DC resistance does not determine high-frequency losses.

The wound copper mass is about 32.2 g. Tube, varnish, end caps, toroid, and hardware are additional.

04 / Design calculations

From winding geometry to frequency

Secondary inductance

The wire centerline diameter is 60 + 0.14224 = 60.14224 mm; radius is 30.07112 mm.

h = N × pitch = 1,500 × 0.14224 = 213.36 mm
Ls[µH] = r²N² / [25.4 × (9r + 10h)]
Wheeler single-layer solenoid; r and h in millimeters.
Ls ≈ 33.317 mH

Copper length, resistance, and mass

ℓ ≈ N × π × mean diameter = 283.41 m
A = π × 0.127² / 4 = 0.012668 mm²
Rdc,20°C = 0.017241 × ℓ / A ≈ 385.7 Ω
m = ℓ[m] × A[mm²] × 8.96 ≈ 32.17 g

Assumed copper resistivity: 0.017241 Ω·mm²/m at 20 °C. Density: 8.96 g/cm³. Skin and proximity losses are not represented by DC resistance.

Capacitances and secondary resonance

Cs[pF] = 11.26h + 16r + 76.4r^(3/2)/√h ≈ 3.746 pF
Empirical approximation reproduced by Skeldon et al.; h and r in meters.
Ct[pF] = 1.4 × (1.2781 − d/D) × √[πd(D − d)] ≈ 6.554 pF
Bert Pool approximation reproduced by Denicolai; toroid diameters in inches.
Ctotal ≈ Cs + Ct + Cextra = 3.746 + 6.554 + 1 = 11.300 pF
fs = 1 / [2π√(Ls Ctotal)] ≈ 259.38 kHz
This preliminary lumped model does not resolve electrostatic shielding, support and PVC dielectric effects, or distributed winding current. Measure the final resonance with the toroid installed.

Required primary inductance

The isolated primary target is assumed to be 10% below the estimated secondary frequency. The 0.90 factor is an adjustable starting point.

fp,target = 0.90 × fs ≈ 233.44 kHz
Lp,total = 1 / [(2πfp)²Cp] ≈ 6.836 µH
Assumed lead inductance = 0.300 µH
Target spiral inductance ≈ 6.536 µH
Z0 = √(Lp,total/Cp) ≈ 10.03 Ω

Z0 is characteristic tank impedance, rather than loss resistance. Coupling produces two normal modes; isolated LC frequencies do not fully describe operation.

07 / Project integration

Remaining construction checks

Geometry and support

The 166 mm primary exceeds a 130–136 mm base. Enlarge its support or recalculate the spiral. The approximate 14.9 mm geometric radial gap to the secondary has not been validated for insulation.

PLA was specified for printed parts. Support temperature, heatsink attachment, and load capacity remain unverified. Calculated copper mass is not complete coil mass.

This notebook shows the PCB and proposed resonator; the enclosure remains a concept.

Measurement sequence

  1. Confirm actual tube diameter, finished wire diameter, and toroid dimensions.
  2. Measure secondary resonance with the toroid fitted using low signal and leaving the bridge unpowered.
  3. Measure the tank including actual leads and adjust the primary tap.
  4. Establish peak current, pulse width, losses, protection, and IGBT switching.
  5. Check capacitor RMS current and dV/dt capability, insulation, and heating for the intended operating regime.
Scope: these calculations do not establish admissible power, output voltage, spark length, or interrupter settings. CPR1's 3 kV DC rating alone does not verify its AC stress. Tuning estimates do not authorize direct mains testing.
