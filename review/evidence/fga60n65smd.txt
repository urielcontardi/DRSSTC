General Description
                                                                                                                        E
  Using Novel Field Stop IGBT Technology, onsemi’s new series
of Field Stop IGBTs offer the optimum performance for Solar
Inverter, UPS, SMPS, Welder and PFC applications where low
conduction and switching losses are essential.

Features                                                                                         G
                                                                                                     C
• Maximum Junction Temperature: TJ = 175 °C                                                              E
• Positive Temperature Co-efficient for Easy Parallel Operating                           TO-3P-3LD / EIAJ SC-65, ISOLATED
• High Current Capability                                                                           CASE 340BZ
• Low Saturation Voltage: VCE(sat) = 1.9 V(Typ.) @ IC = 60 A
• Fast Switching: EOFF = 7.5 mJ/A                                                                MARKING DIAGRAM
• Tighten Parameter Distribution
• This Device is Pb-Free and is RoHS Compliant
Applications
                                                                                                             FGA60N
• Solar Inverter, UPS, SMPS, Welder, PFC                                                                     65SMD
